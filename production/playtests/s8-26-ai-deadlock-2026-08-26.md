# A third of games deadlock, and it is not the AP change

> **Found by**: the S8-24 smoke check, run before S8-01.
> **Status**: ⛔ **ROOT-CAUSED, NOT FIXED.** Needs a decision.
> Raw: `production/qa/evidence/s8-26-deadlock-sim-raw.txt`

## ⛔ The finding

**10 of 30 AI-vs-AI matches (33%) end in a total deadlock.** In those games, measured at the end:

| | Stalled games | Healthy games |
|---|---|---|
| Peak army size, either side | ⛔ **1 unit** | 6–7 units |
| Credits banked at the end | ⛔ **~24,000** | 350–6,250 |
| HQ damage | ⛔ **none — 40/40 both sides** | decisive |
| Ends by | the round cap, on a unit-count tiebreak | a win |

**Neither side ever fields more than one unit.** They sit on tens of thousands of credits with
untouched bases until the clock runs out.

## ✅ It is NOT the AP change — I got that wrong first

The smoke check reported this as a consequence of `flat_ap_per_turn` 30 → 20 (S8-23), comparing 33%
against S7-12's 9% baseline. **That attribution was wrong.** Re-running the identical batch with the
old values:

| Configuration | Reach the cap | True deadlocks |
|---|---:|---:|
| 20 AP / carry 10 | 11/30 (37%) | 10 |
| **30 AP / carry 15** | **10/30 (33%)** | **10** |

⇒ **Identical.** The AP change is exonerated. ★ Raising `VS_MAX_ROUNDS` 40 → 60 also does not help —
games simply run to the new cap, which is itself the tell: they are not *slow*, they are **stuck**.

## ★ The mechanism

`tools/DiagnoseAI.tscn`, per turn, on both sides:

```
turn 1:  seq = ["PRODUCE(Builder)", "BUILD"]     2 actions
turn 2:  seq = []                                 0 actions  — HQ on cooldown
turn 3:  seq = ["PRODUCE(Builder)", "BUILD"]     ...
```

with `best_move = -1.000`, `best_attack = -1.000`, `best_build = -1.000` at the start of every turn —
**the only candidate the AI ever has is producing another Builder**, and the rejected follow-up is
`PRODUCE → PRODUCER_ON_COOLDOWN`.

⇒ **The AI raises a Barracks and then never produces a single fighting unit from it.** It loops
HQ → Builder → Barracks forever, banking every credit it earns, with no army and nothing to attack
with.

⚠ **This is the S8-13 production rework, not the AI's scoring weights.** Before S8-13 the HQ made
Scouts directly, so an army existed from turn 1. Now the HQ makes **only** Builders and every
fighting unit comes from a Barracks — and the AI never took the second step.
★ **S8-17 fixed the half of this that was visible** (build-then-cancel-for-the-refund). The
remaining half was hidden behind it: the AI stopped demolishing its buildings, and simply stopped
*using* them instead.

## ⛔ Why it blocks S8-01

**Roughly one game in three, the opponent does nothing at all.** The player would face an empty
board for 60 rounds and win on a tiebreak. That answers none of the six questions, and it
specifically destroys **Analysis A** — *"did the game feel yours to lose?"* — which needs an
opponent that plays.

## ⚠ Two instrument bugs found on the way, both now fixed

1. ⛔ **`diagnose_ai.gd` passed `committed` — the count of ALL actions this turn — into
   `AI.choose_action`'s `economy_investments_committed` parameter.** That inflated the economy
   cadence counter, so the AI believed it had exhausted its economy budget and stopped producing.
   ★★ **The tool that exists to diagnose "why did the AI pass with its budget unspent" was
   manufacturing exactly that symptom.** `simulate_matches.gd` and `diagnose_cliff.gd` both pass a
   real counter, which is why the batch numbers were trustworthy and the trace was not.
   ⚠ It also bailed out of a turn on the *first* rejected candidate, where the real driver retries —
   making an ordinary rejection look like a terminal stall.
2. ⚠ **`SIM_END`'s `capped` field compares against the harness's `MAX_TURNS = 200`, not the game's
   `VS_MAX_ROUNDS`** — so it reads 0 for every game and means nothing. Already documented in
   `.agent/notes.md`; reading that note is the only reason the first "0% hit the cap" reading was
   caught before it shipped as a finding.

★ **Both are the same shape as the S8-19/S8-14 guard defects: the instrument was wrong in a way
that produced a confident, plausible, false reading.** Distrust a clean result; distrust a dramatic
one just as much.

## Options

| | Cost | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Teach the AI to produce from a Barracks** | Real AI work, unknown depth | ★ The actual fix. It is a gap in candidate generation or scoring, not a tuning value |
| **Let the HQ also make one fighting unit** | Small | ⚠ Partly reverts the S8-13 design the user chose |
| **Play anyway** | Free | ⛔ **Not recommended** — a 1-in-3 chance of a dead opponent in a session that has already rolled seven times |

# ✅ FIXED — S8-27, 2026-08-26

## The mechanism, exactly

Once each side's Barracks completed, every turn on both sides:

```
seq = ["PRODUCE(Sniper)", "ATTACK((5,5)->(6,5))"]   pop = 1/6
seq = ["PRODUCE(Sniper)", "ATTACK((6,5)->(5,5))"]   pop = 1/6
```

Both AIs build their Barracks **toward the middle**, so their deploy rings end up **adjacent**.
Production scoring rewards forward tiles through `_reachability_multiplier` and had **no
counterweight for the unit dying immediately** — so each side produced one Sniper per turn
(attack 6 against a Sniper's 3 hp: an instant one-shot), traded it, and repeated forever.
Population never exceeded 1 on either side.

★ **Not a scoring-weight problem and not a Barracks-candidate bug** — both of which I suspected
first. The AI *was* producing from the Barracks, correctly, every turn. It was producing into a
grave.

## The fix

`_deploy_tile_is_lethal()` — would an enemy **kill this unit outright**, before it ever acts?
If so, `deploy_into_death_penalty` (0.15) scales the candidate's value down.

- ★ **A multiplier, not a veto.** If every deploy tile is threatened — a base under siege — a veto
  would leave the AI unable to produce **at all**, a worse deadlock than the one being fixed.
- ★ Uses `Combat.preview_damage` against a provisional `UnitState`, not a raw `attack` vs `hp`
  comparison, so cover and defense resolve exactly as the real exchange would (CR-4: the AI reasons
  with the numbers the game resolves with).
- ⚠ Fires only on an **outright kill**. Deploying into danger is often correct; deploying into
  guaranteed death before the unit acts never is.

## Result — 30 matches, same seeds and board

| | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| ⛔ **Deadlocked games** | **10/30 (33%)** | ✅ **0/30** |
| Median match length | 16 rounds | 27 rounds |
| Median credits banked at the end | 4,800 | 2,650 |

★ Games now **play out** rather than stalling: longer, and the AI spends what it earns instead of
hoarding it. Suite 1307/1307.
