## ⛔⛔ THE ORIGINAL (INCORRECT) FINDING — the AP change made a third of games end on the clock

**30 AI-vs-AI matches at the new `flat_ap_per_turn = 20`, shipped 40-round cap:**

| | 30 AP (S7-12 baseline) | ⚠ **20 AP (now)** |
|---|---:|---:|
| Games resolving **on play** | 20/22 — **91%** | 20/30 — **67%** |
| Games decided by the **round cap** | 2/22 — **9%** | ⛔ **10/30 — 33%** |
| Nearly-even (+1) cell reaching the cap | **0/6** | (not isolated this run) |

⇒ **A 3.6× increase in games decided by the timer rather than by play.**

### Why, and why it matters for the play session

**Mechanically obvious in hindsight:** fewer AP per turn means fewer actions, which means less
damage per turn, which means slower attrition and longer games. The cap did not move; the game got
slower underneath it.

⛔ **A capped game is decided by a unit-count tiebreak, not by winning.** That is exactly what
S7-12 raised the cap to *stop* — its own words: *"the cap was deciding games that should have been
decided on play, and it was doing so selectively… truncating exactly the games worth watching."*

★★ **This lands directly on Analysis A** — *"does the swing feel alive; did the game feel yours to
lose?"* A game that ends on a timer and is awarded on unit count feels like **neither** a win nor a
loss. At roughly one game in three, there is a real chance the session's headline question gets
answered by an artefact of the cap rather than by the design.

⚠ **Caveat, stated honestly:** this is **AI vs AI**. A human plays more decisively than this AI and
may well close games faster, so 33% is a ceiling on the risk, not a prediction. **It is a flag, not
a blocker.**

### Options, in order of cost

1. **Play anyway and watch for it.** If a game ends on the cap, that itself is a finding — note it
   in the session file. ★ Cheapest, and keeps the session on schedule.
2. **Scale the cap with the pace.** Each turn now does ~⅔ the work, so ~1.5× the rounds are needed
   for the same match. `VS_MAX_ROUNDS` 40 → **~60** would restore the proportion resolving on play.
   ⚠ Costs match length — a capped game already runs 80 turns at 40 rounds.
3. **Lower the `*_ap_cost` surcharges instead.** ★ This is the dial `economy_config.gd` explicitly
   recommends for tuning AP scarcity, and it buys more actions per turn without touching the budget.
4. **Revert to 30 AP.** Restores every S7-09…S7-17 measurement at the cost of the experiment.
