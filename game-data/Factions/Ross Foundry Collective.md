## Notes

★ `design/gdd/factions/machinists-union.md`. Poor at the start (−300 base income) with the steepest economy slope (+200 per tier); infantry that are mostly crew (a Machinist makes its vehicle hit +1 and move 1 cheaper), five heavy vehicles, the toughest Factory (3 allowed) and the Bulwark, the strongest defence in the game.

★ 2026-10-01 (user decision): every Economy-tree tech is now an income tier (+500/+800/+1,200), and Ross's slope is +500 per tier (was +200). Its income runs 1,200 → 5,200 against the baseline's 1,500 → 4,000 — the late-game payoff its design always promised. The +200 slope left Ross at 35–37% because it climbs the tree slowest (it is poorest early).
